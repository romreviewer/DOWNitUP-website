About DOWNitUP

The free, cross-platform download manager and torrent client

Why we built DOWNitUP

Downloading large files should be simple, fast, and free. Yet the most popular download manager on Windows, Internet Download Manager (IDM), is paid, Windows-only, and ships with an interface that has barely changed in a decade. The free alternatives often bundle adware, nag you with offers, or leave out major platforms.

We built DOWNitUP to be the download manager we wanted ourselves: genuinely free, modern, private, and available on every platform we use — Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android. It combines multi-connection HTTP acceleration (the same technique IDM uses to speed up downloads) with a built-in BitTorrent client, so one app handles both regular file downloads and magnet links.

What we believe

  • Free means free. No trial periods, no license keys, no feature locks, no bundled offers during installation.
  • Privacy is not a premium feature. Your download history and data stay on your device. DOWNitUP collects no personal data and includes no third-party trackers. Read our privacy policy for the details.
  • One app, every platform. A download manager should follow you across your desktop and your phone, with a consistent, modern experience everywhere.
  • Speed matters. Multi-connection downloading, smart resume, and efficient transfers are the core of the product, not an afterthought.

The project

DOWNitUP is developed by an independent developer under the RomReviewer Tools label. The Android app is available on Google Play, and desktop builds for Windows, macOS, and Linux are published on GitHub, where releases are versioned and distributed openly.

Editorial policy

The guides and comparisons on our blog are written to be genuinely useful, not just to promote our app. When we compare DOWNitUP to other download managers like IDM, FDM, or qBittorrent, we aim to describe each product's real strengths and weaknesses honestly — including cases where a competitor is the better fit. If you spot an inaccuracy in any article, we want to hear about it.
